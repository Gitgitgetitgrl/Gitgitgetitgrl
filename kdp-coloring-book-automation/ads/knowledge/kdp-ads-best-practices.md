# KDP Ads Best Practices (Ada's strategy baseline)

_Seeded July 2026 from market research (Amazon KDP advertising tactics). Update via dated
notes in `trend-notes/` — newer notes override this baseline._

Amazon KDP advertising (Sponsored Products primarily) is one of the most direct ways to
drive visibility and sales for self-published books, including coloring books. It works
especially well for niches with searchable demand. Success depends on **testing,
optimization, and realistic expectations** — positive ROI requires ongoing management.
**ACoS target: 30–50% or lower** for profitability (royalties typically ~60% print / ~70%
ebook minus costs).

## Core ad types

- **Sponsored Products** — most common and effective for books; appears in search results
  and product pages. **The starting point.**
- **Sponsored Brands** — brand/series awareness (logo + multiple books).
- **Display/video** — broader reach, less common for most KDP; Sponsored Products drive
  the bulk of direct sales.

## Proven tactics (2026 best practices)

### 1. Start with automatic campaigns (discovery)
Let Amazon's algorithm test broad targeting. Run **1–2 weeks** to gather conversion data,
then **harvest winning search terms and ASINs** for manual campaigns. Key for new
books/niches.

### 2. Transition to manual targeting
- **Keyword targeting:** exact, phrase, and broad match. Focus **long-tail specific terms**
  (e.g. "bold and easy coloring book for seniors", "cottagecore coloring book") over generic
  ("adult coloring book"). Use **negative keywords** to prevent wasted spend.
- **Product/ASIN targeting:** bid on similar successful books (top-BSR coloring books).
- **Category targeting:** broader reach in Hobbies/Crafts or Children's sections.

### 3. Bidding & budget management
- **Dynamic bids — down only** initially (safety).
- Daily budgets **$5–20/day per campaign**; scale winners.
- Adjust bids on performance: raise high-conversion terms, lower/pause losers. Aim for
  break-even or better within weeks.
- Coloring books: test **seasonal boosts** (holidays) and niche-specific terms.

### 4. Optimization routine (weekly/monthly)
- Review: **search-term reports, ACoS, CTR, conversion rate, impressions**.
- Pause high-spend/low-sales keywords; **promote high-performers to exact match**.
- Monitor relevance — irrelevant clicks kill profitability.
- Tools: Publisher Rocket, Helium 10, Amazon's built-in analytics.

### 5. Listing optimization FIRST (pre-ads)
Ads **amplify** existing listings. Strong titles, descriptions, backend keywords,
eye-catching covers (show sample pages for coloring books), A+ Content, and reviews boost
conversion dramatically. **Poor listings waste ad spend.**

### 6. Launch & scaling
- **New launches:** run ads early — Amazon's algorithm favors velocity. Combine with price
  promotions when possible.
- **Budget split:** ~**70/30 auto/manual**; test multiple campaigns.
- **Reader magnets:** bonuses (extra pages, printable downloads) encourage reviews/repeat buys.
- **Series strategy:** advertise **book 1** to funnel into the series.

### 7. Niche tips (coloring books)
- Target **"Bold and Easy"**, breed-specific, cozy, or wellness terms — lower competition.
- Emphasize **relaxation/stress-relief** in copy and targeting.
- Optimized niches perform; generics struggle.

## Pitfalls & ROI reality

- **High ACoS early is normal** during testing — refine aggressively (real cases scale from
  unprofitable to ~45% ACoS with optimization and hundreds of monthly sales).
- **Low margins:** print coloring books have production costs — compute true profitability
  (royalty − ad cost per sale).
- **No quick wins:** expect $100s–$1,000s spent on data early; consistent winners see
  returns over time. Refine over **4–8 weeks**.
- **Compliance:** follow Amazon's policies (no misleading claims); some niches restricted.

## Advanced

- Separate **evergreen vs. promotional** campaigns.
- **Amazon Marketing Cloud (AMC)** for audience building at scale.
- Combine with **external traffic** (email lists, social) for organic lift.
- Track **TACoS** (total ACoS incl. organic sales) for the full picture.

**Bottom line:** start small with auto campaigns, learn, double down on winners. Niche
focus + visual appeal give coloring books an edge. Monitor daily/weekly; expect refinement
over 4–8 weeks.
